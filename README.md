# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Overview

An interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI** to analyze employee workforce, attrition, demographics, salary, job satisfaction, and professional experience.

The dashboard provides key HR metrics and interactive visualizations to help understand workforce distribution and employee attrition patterns.

## 🎯 Objective

* Analyze overall employee workforce
* Measure employee attrition and attrition rate
* Analyze department-wise employee distribution
* Identify attrition patterns across salary slabs
* Analyze employee distribution by age group and gender
* Understand job satisfaction across different job roles
* Explore attrition based on employee experience

## 📂 Dataset

The project uses an HR employee dataset containing information related to:

* Employee demographics
* Department and job role
* Salary and compensation
* Job satisfaction
* Performance
* Work experience
* Employee attrition

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Power BI**
* **Power Query**
* **DAX**
* **Data Visualization**
* **CSV Dataset**

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights

### Key KPIs

* **Total Employees:** 1,417
* **Active Employees:** 1,186
* **Attrition Count:** 231
* **Attrition Rate:** 16.3%
* **Average Age:** 36.94
* **Average Experience:** 7.04 years

### Analysis Included

* Attrition by Department
* Attrition by Salary Slab
* Job Role vs Job Satisfaction
* Age Group Distribution
* Attrition by Gender
* Attrition by Total Experience
* Department-wise Employee Count

## 🔍 Key Insights

* The overall employee **attrition rate is 16.3%**.
* The **26–35 age group** has the highest employee count.
* **Operations** has the largest employee population.
* The **6–10 LPA salary slab** shows the highest displayed attrition count.
* Male employees have a higher absolute number of attritions than female employees.
* Attrition is more prominent among employees with relatively lower experience.
